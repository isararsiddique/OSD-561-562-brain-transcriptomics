Figure 3. Multialternative compositions and a coordinate-defined candidate. a) Every FLT–GC cluster in the joint 2,769-test family; blue denotes BY q<0.05 and the dashed line q=0.05. b) Global equal-cluster contrasts from square-root animal compositions; scaled distance is sqrt(T/2), not a percentage or the Hellinger distance between pooled group means. Exact whole-animal allocations and three-test Holm correction are as in Fig. 1. c) All 26 animals at Tcaf1 cluster 6:2:42663893:start; dots are individual animals and segments group means (FLT n=9, GC n=9, VIV n=8). Coordinates denote inclusive STAR intron ends, not exon coordinates; this minus-strand boundary shares an acceptor and varies donor-side support. The native-composition changes are descriptive components of the corrected cluster test, not separately tested alternatives. Tcaf1 passes the primary FLT–GC and FLT–VIV BY tests, but its FLT–VIV result does not survive the 95% dominance sensitivity. The selection of Tcaf1 for illustration is post hoc.
