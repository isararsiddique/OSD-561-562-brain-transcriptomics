Supplementary Figure 5. Additional retrospective robustness checks for frozen RR23 multiway junction compositions. a) The original 43 BY-positive contrast–cluster tests (41 distinct clusters) decrease to 25 under the 95% dominance gate; all ten original A-half datasets have zero BY-positive tests. The family remains 2,769, with failed measurements assigned p=1. The 30 global comparisons across these original A-half draws all survive Holm within their replicate; this aggregate stability coexists with depth-sensitive local findings. b) Tcaf1 is valid in all ten original A-half draws, but both original local comparisons lose BY significance in these A halves. Its flight–vivarium q also rises from 0.0451 to 0.0538 under the 95% gate. All original A-half draws and the original/95% values are shown; no favorable replicate was selected. c) Observed global distances with pointwise 95% confidence-ball-derived intervals from 2,000 within-arm whole-animal bootstraps. The bootstrap centroid-error norm supplies the error radius. The intervals are approximate, not simultaneously corrected, and do not replace exact tests; nonnegative distance percentile intervals are not used to assert significance. d) Sample-size-unbiased within-group mean squared Hellinger radius, with pointwise bootstrap percentile ranges. Diagnostic Holm values follow flight–ground, flight–vivarium, ground–vivarium order. Dispersion label references recompute centroids under each assignment and require exchangeability of complete distributions; location differences invalidate an interpretation as an exact equal-dispersion test. Studentization does not guarantee small-sample validity under unequal covariance. Flight collection date (14 January 2021) is confounded with its condition versus both ground arms (17 January). These are repeated analyses of the same animals and cannot establish full isoforms, protein effects, injury, or causal flight biology. The complementary B-half results are reported separately in main Fig. 5; this panel does not summarize all 20 halves.
