Figure 4. Exact transcript context and empirical read support at Tcaf1. a) Every matching transcript for the three measured introns is shown. The minus-strand introns share genomic start 42,663,893; spans are 5,974, 22,570 and 23,000 bp. Filled endpoints mark the protein-coding Tcaf1-201/-202/-203 matches, whose exonic junction boundaries lie in the 5′ UTR. Other matches remain visible: Tcaf1-205/-206 are retained_intron and Tcaf1-208 is protein_coding_CDS_not_defined. Labels give donor→acceptor exon ranks; lines show intron spans, not complete transcripts. b) The three protein-coding matches share identical annotated coding coordinates and phases, 2,766 CDS bases and CCDS20076. Joined CDS blocks follow minus-strand transcript order; digits are GTF phases. The other matched transcripts lack a CDS annotation. These are reference relationships, not measured protein sequences. c) Every animal's 23,000-bp intron fraction and total selected counts across all three alternatives; FLT n=9, GC n=9, VIV n=8. This coverage relation is descriptive, without a new significance test. d) All 26 animals support the companion intron 6:2:42686572:42686892 with 20–169 unique reads; segments are means. Its support does not establish intron retention in a full RNA molecule. The complete audit retains 1,199/3,248 multiway junctions without exact GTF intron matches, although they passed the original STAR annotation-status filter. Tcaf1 was selected post hoc from the complete corrected family; its flight–vivarium BY result fails the 95% sensitivity and neither contrast survives half-depth BY correction. All read measurements reuse the same libraries. Flight and collection date are aliased (14 versus 17 January 2021); no causal flight, complete-isoform or protein consequence is inferred.
