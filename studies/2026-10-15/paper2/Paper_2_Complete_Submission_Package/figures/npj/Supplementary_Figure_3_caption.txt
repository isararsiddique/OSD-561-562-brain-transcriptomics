Supplementary Figure 3. Gene abundance does not establish an independent junction phenotype. a) FLT–GC effects for all 1,977 jointly measurable genes; the rank correlation is descriptive and has no independent-gene significance test. b) All FLT–GC junction-association/abundance-equivalence conjunction q values, sorted separately at each margin. The conjunction p is the maximum of the exact junction p and the Welch equivalence p; BY correction covers 5,931 tests separately for each margin. No conjunction passes BY at either margin. These points summarize gene-level effects and corrected tests; they are not biological replicates. Relative abundance used the publisher-verified STAR matrix, technical-library aggregation, median-ratio normalization and log2(normalized count+0.5).
