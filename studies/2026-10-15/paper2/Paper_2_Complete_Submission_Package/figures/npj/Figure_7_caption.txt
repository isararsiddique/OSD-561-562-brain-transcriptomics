Figure 7. Retrospective count-model sensitivity of frozen RR23 junction clusters. a, Standard Dirichlet–multinomial (DM) parameters were fitted to 26 animal count vectors for each of 923 clusters without using condition labels. The pooled intraclass overdispersion includes all between-animal heterogeneity; it is not a within-condition biological variance estimate. Tcaf1 is marked. b, Flight–ground exact evidence from the original square-root-composition test and pooled-covariance-whitened DM mean-logit scores. Every fixed-size animal assignment was enumerated; orange points pass BY in both complete 2,769-test families. The open circle marks Tcaf1, which overlaps other minimum-p points. Rank correlation is descriptive and does not establish independent replication. c, BY-positive contrast–cluster tests under the original analysis, DM scores, the multinomial-limit score, DM with the stricter 95% dominance gate, and DM in each of the ten original A-half count thinnings. These are separate sensitivity families; discoveries are not pooled across methods. Failed measurement gates retain p=1. All ten original A-half draws have zero local BY findings, while all 30 global DM contrasts in these A-half draws remain Holm-positive within replicate. d, Tcaf1 retains flight–ground significance with both count-score constructions, but its flight–vivarium comparison does not. All 20 full-depth DM discoveries are tied at the minimum exact p; losing one of these minimum-p tests under the stricter gate raises the minimum BY q from 0.04843 to 0.05098 without changing retained raw p values. No threshold was changed after observing this boundary. Count-model fits and score calibration are not a DRIMSeq/LeafCutter implementation, independent replication, or causal adjustment. Exact reference calibration requires animal exchangeability; condition remains confounded with collection date.
