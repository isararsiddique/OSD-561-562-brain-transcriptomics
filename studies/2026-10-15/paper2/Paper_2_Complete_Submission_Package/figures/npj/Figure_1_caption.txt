Figure 1. Animal-level design and aggregate junction differences. a) RR23 cerebellar source animals and collection dates; technical libraries are not biological replicates. b) Principal components of all 5,249 measured binary choices, centered across 26 animals without selection by test results. c) All animal retained unique-junction depths; segments show group means. d) RMS differences in group mean fractions over the same 5,249 choices. Exact whole-animal allocations enumerate 48,620 FLT–GC and 24,310 assignments in each 9-versus-8 comparison, with Holm correction across three global tests. These are effect summaries, not bars hiding biological variation; the animal distributions appear in b and c. FLT, flight; GC, habitat ground control; VIV, vivarium control. FLT n=9, GC n=9, VIV n=8. Collection-date aliasing limits causal interpretation.
