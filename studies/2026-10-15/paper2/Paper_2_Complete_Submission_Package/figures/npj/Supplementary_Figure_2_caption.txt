Supplementary Figure 2. Dependence and read-depth diagnostics for binary choices. a) Effect-profile correlations for all 126 distinct partitions of the nine habitat controls into disjoint sets of four and five. The blue line uses the same controls in both contrasts; the dashed line is the median over disjoint partitions. Partitions reuse animals and are not independent replicates. b) All ten binomial depth thinnings of the full count matrix, with frozen binary definitions; dashed lines show unthinned effects. Target retained depth is 16,249,784 reads. All 30 global tests retain Holm significance. These diagnostics assess mathematical dependence and read sampling; they do not provide new biological replication or remove collection-date aliasing.
