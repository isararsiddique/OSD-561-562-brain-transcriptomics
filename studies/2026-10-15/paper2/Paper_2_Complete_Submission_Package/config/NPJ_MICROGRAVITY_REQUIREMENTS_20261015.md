## Manuscript
- Initial submission is format-flexible. The detailed section/end-matter arrangement below describes the publisher's preparation guidance, rather than a prohibition on an integrated reading copy.
- Article title: up to 15 words, free of punctuation, idioms and puns.
- Abstract: no subheadings, no more than 150 words.
- No strict total word/page limit; write concisely.
- Introduction and Discussion: no subheadings. Discussion includes limitations in prose, with no separate Limitations or Conclusions section.
- Results and Methods use subheadings. All methods in main text; no Supplementary Methods.
- Statistics and reproducibility heading: units, sample sizes, exclusions, tests, assumptions, tails, multiplicity, actual p values and variability/uncertainty.
- Disclose LLM assistance in Methods. LLMs are not authors.
- Data availability required; Code availability where applicable. Contributions use author initials.
- Funding within Acknowledgements; no separate Funding section. Competing interests must state actual financial and nonfinancial information.
- References numbered by first appearance; Nature style; 60 suggested, not a strict limit.
- Figure legends after references, tables at end. Each combined figure and legend on one page for the submitted PDF. Legend maximum 350 words.
- Supplementary Information in one PDF. Large tabular files are Supplementary Data XX, with titles/legends in that PDF.

## Figures
- Combined panels on one page, lowercase bold a), b), c), consistent scale and fonts.
- White background; Arial or Helvetica; optimum 8 pt at final size. Lines at least 1 pt.
- Prepare at final printed size. A4-page-width figures preferred; our figures use 183 mm.
- RGB at least 300 dpi; editable vector PDF/SVG preferred. Our raster exports use 600 dpi.
- Clear colourblind-accessible palette, no rainbow/red-green encoding, unnecessary decoration or chart junk.
- Show full data distributions for biological replicates; explain error bars. No mean-only bars that conceal distributions.
- No graphical abstract requirement or prescribed graphical-abstract dimensions found on either verified page. The provided graphical summary is optional artwork, not an inferred submission requirement.
