# Scope of the contribution and prior art

Checked 15 October 2026. This is a focused review, not an exhaustive systematic priority search.

Henrich et al. (2022), DOI 10.1186/s13395-022-00294-9, already studied spaceflight-related alternative splicing in skeletal muscle, including RNA-binding proteins and comparison with gene abundance. Its full public article was read. A general claim that spaceflight splicing or RNA-regulatory candidates are new is unsupported.

Masarapu et al. (2024), DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-48916-8, reports spatially resolved brain multiomics and neuronal/glial processes; the indexed abstract was reviewed. It is related brain biology, not independent validation of these local cerebellar junctions.

Kremsky et al. (2023), DOI 10.3390/ijms241713569, reports spatial brain gene-expression responses and antioxidant treatment; its full public article was read. It documents a three-day ground protocol offset. Regional expression findings do not resolve the present local transcript architecture or flight/date alias.

LeafCutter and rMATS establish junction- and event-based splicing methods. Our square-root composition and Gram-kernel exact calculations combine established mathematical tools; they should not be presented as a newly invented class of algorithm without methodological benchmarking.

The specific contribution is the joint resource: an all-animal, label-invariant three-arm cerebellar panel; full animal-level exact inference; conservative gene and multialternative localization; exact transcript-context interpretation; full-family depth robustness; and frozen independent-mission transport. The strongest biological follow-up is the coordinate-defined Tcaf1 alternative 5′ architecture, with the coding-structure and failed-transport limits retained.

The focused title/abstract query for spaceflight or microgravity, splicing/isoform, and brain/cerebellum/neural returned two tangential records. A TCAF1/TRPM8 and spaceflight/microgravity title/abstract query returned no records. Neither absence proves novelty: indexing coverage, synonyms and unpublished work limit a negative search. Broad RR23 queries produced unrelated records and are not evidence of a missing literature. Exact queries/results, verified DOI metadata and available full texts are archived in config and extensions/transcript_context/literature.

Permitted wording: “coordinate-defined cerebellar candidates”, “alternative first-exon architecture”, “comparator-dependent associations”, and “limited observed transport”. Avoid “first”, “previously undiscovered mechanism”, “abundance independent”, “protein switching”, “causal microgravity effect” or “validated neurological biomarker”.
