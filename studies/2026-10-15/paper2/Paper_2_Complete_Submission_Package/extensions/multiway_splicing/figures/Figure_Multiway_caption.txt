Figure MW1. Retrospective multiway local junction-composition analysis of RR23 bulk cerebellum. (A) Selection applied once to all 26 animals without group labels, starting from canonical annotated junctions. The denominator in the dominance gate contains every retained canonical junction at that boundary, including alternatives failing the five-read/three-animal gate. Substantive pooled composition means each alternative ≥1%, at least three alternatives ≥5%, and maximum ≤90%. (B) Principal coordinates of the centered PSD equal-cluster sqrt-composition kernel; all animals are shown, and the displayed axes explain 11.4% and 7.1% of its variance. (C) Observed centroid Hellinger distances (diamonds) against all 48,620, 24,310, and 24,310 assignments, respectively. The interval spans the central 95% of permutation statistics and is not a confidence interval for the effect. Holm p values correct the three global comparisons. (D) A post hoc example, the only one of three flight–vivarium BY discoveries mapping every alternative to one gene, Tcaf1 (ENSMUSG00000036667). Animal dots and mean ticks show each alternative in all three arms. Its three introns share genomic start 42,663,893 on chromosome 6, negative strand; their ends are printed on the x axis. The event was selected for display after complete-family inference; it is not independent confirmation. All group compositions, junction coordinates, local tests, and null results are supplied. Flight collection date is confounded with its condition, and no complete-isoform, protein, cell-intrinsic, injury, or causal-flight claim follows.
