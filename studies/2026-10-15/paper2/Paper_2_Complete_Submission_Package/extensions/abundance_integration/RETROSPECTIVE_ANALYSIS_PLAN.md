# Gene abundance and local junction usage

Recorded 15 October 2026 (local date), before fitting abundance comparisons. The junction-level and gene-omnibus outcomes were already known. This is a retrospective, complementary analysis, not preregistered or independent replication.

Use the deposited OSD-525 STAR gene-count matrix after publisher MD5 verification. Sum technical libraries by the same source-animal mapping used for junctions. Estimate DESeq-style median-of-ratios size factors using genes positive in all 26 animals; normalize all animals without group labels and analyze log2(normalized count + 0.5). Restrict the testing universe to the pre-existing 1,983 uniquely mapped measured junction genes that have at least 20 raw gene reads in every animal. Preserve and report every excluded gene.

Enumerate all animal label allocations for each of FLT–GC, GC–VIV, FLT–VIV. Use absolute mean log2 contrast; move complete animal vectors. Joint BH and BY corrections include all three gene-abundance contrast families. A separate three-test Holm family tests global mean squared log-abundance contrasts. Normalization is held fixed under permutations because it does not use group labels. Log-count results quantify relative abundance under this normalization, not absolute transcription.

As explicitly exploratory sensitivity analyses, report Welch confidence intervals and two one-sided equivalence tests at margins ±log2(1.2) and ±log2(1.5). Correct equivalence jointly over all measured genes and three contrasts at each declared margin. Also use max(junction omnibus p, abundance-equivalence p) as a conjunction p value with joint BY correction at each margin. These analyses depend on approximate Welch inference as well as the exact junction null; neither nonsignificant abundance nor a positive equivalence test demonstrates mechanistic independence or absence of all abundance change. Report both margins regardless of outcome.
