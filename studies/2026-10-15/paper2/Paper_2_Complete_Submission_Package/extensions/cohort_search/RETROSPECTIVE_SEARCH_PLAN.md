# Frozen retrospective cohort search plan

Date: 2026-10-15 (Asia/Calcutta). This is a new retrospective search after RR23 and RRRM2 results were known, not a preregistration. Cohort eligibility is frozen here before retrieval of new expression or junction outcomes.

Purpose: identify additional biologically independent mouse cerebellar spaceflight RNA-sequencing cohorts with flight and suitable ground controls, and document all screened candidates. The primary source is NASA OSDR public metadata/file inventory, supplemented by existing workspace inventories and Europe PMC literature. Search terms: cerebellum, cerebellar, brain, spaceflight, rodent, mouse; local catalog screening is followed by live API confirmation. Record queries, URLs, retrieval times, response hashes and failures. A bounded search cannot prove absence across all public repositories.

Primary eligibility: Mus musculus; true spaceflight exposure; cerebellum identified separately; sequencing measurements capable of junction reconstruction (processed STAR SJ counts preferred; raw reads only a future-work option if computationally infeasible); identifiable biological animals with flight and ground comparator; mission and source-animal independence from RR23/OSD-525 and RRRM2/OSD-561. A tissue from the same animals or same mission is not independent-mission replication. Eligibility does not depend on effect size, direction or significance. Report every discovered candidate and reasons for inclusion/exclusion. Match male C57BL/6J, life stage, exposure/collection and preparation where possible; mismatches are measured transport limitations, not grounds for outcome-driven rejection.

Secondary eligibility/context: actual brain or cerebellum from independent missions with different molecular platforms, or other brain regions from existing missions, may support explicitly labelled tissue/mission context. They do not validate local cerebellar junction measurements unless those measurements are observed. Ground analogs, other species, irradiation, culture and non-neural tissues are excluded from primary replication but retained in the decision table if surfaced.

No outcome analysis is authorized by this search module. For suitable available processed data, acquire publisher inventories/checksum sidecars and metadata, assess animal identity and design confounding, and report to the parent before inferential analysis. Unknown metadata means unresolved eligibility. A public annotation date is not a biological collection date. Perfect exposure/collection confounding cannot be repaired statistically without identifying assumptions or new design information.

The script and tables will retain failed searches as well as returned records. No claim of systematic review or universal novelty is intended.
