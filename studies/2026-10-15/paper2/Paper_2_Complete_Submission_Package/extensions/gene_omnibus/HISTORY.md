# Retrospective analysis history

This extension was implemented on 15 October 2026 after the repaired all-animal RR23 event analysis was available. It is an exploratory extension, not a preregistered study.

1. The task defined gene-level tests using every pooled, measured and uniquely intron-mapped event. The gene universe was not selected from event p values or directions. The first implementation designated correction across all three contrasts and all 1,983 genes, retaining arbitrary-dependence BY as the conservative result alongside BH.
2. The first gene-level result contained 47 FLT–habitat GC calls at joint BY q=0.0482569878. Complete unsuccessful tests and zero joint-BY calls in the two vivarium contrasts were retained. The near-threshold, discrete exact-p resolution was disclosed before biological interpretation.
3. Representative Hnrnpa2b1 and Stxbp1 displays, and named scatter-plot candidates, were selected after seeing gene results. They are labeled post hoc illustrations and do not add independent discoveries.
4. The 95% boundary-dominance sensitivity and descriptive animal-deletion analyses were requested after the 47-gene result. The 95% gate was already defined in the shared all26 measurement table. The original 1,983-gene / 5,949-test family was held fixed, with unmeasured genes assigned p=1. No significance threshold, new gene universe, or event weighting was optimized to retain calls.
5. Forty-five of the 47 primary calls retained BY significance under that stricter gate. Rps6kb1 and Ecpas were disclosed as non-retaining; Septin4 and Atosa were disclosed as sensitivity-only calls. The primary set was not replaced by the sensitivity set.
6. All 1,983 genes were assessed under every one of 18 FLT or GC animal omissions. Only descriptive effects and directions were calculated, without smaller-sample p values. The main report gives the 47-gene subset as post hoc stability description and explicitly does not call it replication.

The flight–collection-date alias cannot be resolved by any of these analyses. No experiment, outside validation cohort, complete isoform reconstruction, protein assay, injury endpoint, or causal flight mechanism was added.
