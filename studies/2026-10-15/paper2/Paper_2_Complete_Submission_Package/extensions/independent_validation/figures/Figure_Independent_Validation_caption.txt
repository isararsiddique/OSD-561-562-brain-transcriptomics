Independent-launch validation of the complete RR23 all26-defined junction panel in RRRM2/OSD-561 cerebellum. (A) Measurement transport: 5,724 frozen definitions, 5,249 measured across all 26 RR23 animals, and 1,512 meeting the same all-animal coverage and dominance gates in both studies (28.8% of the RR23 measured panel). RRRM2 failure counts are disjoint. (B) Every common-panel event effect; RR23 FLT−GC means and the RRRM2 animal-count-weighted average of FLT−GC means within route×age strata. Spearman ρ=0.010 and Pearson r=−0.011; the dashed diagonal marks equal effects. (C) Spearman correlation from 2,000 independent whole-animal bootstrap draws within exposure groups in RR23 and exposure×route×age cells in RRRM2; orange lines show the observed correlation and percentile 95% interval [−0.069, 0.074], and the black dashed line marks zero. Events are not treated as independent biological replicates. (D) RR23-only learned directions projected with equal event weights into every held-out RRRM2 animal. Points are animals and bars are group means; a common stratum mean is subtracted only for display. The weighted score contrast is 0.128 percentage points (animal-bootstrap 95% interval −0.137 to 0.422), exact one-sided p=0.2525 over all 11,200 assignments preserving FLT counts within each route×age stratum (two-sided p=0.5022). No RR23 significance, large-effect or direction-positive subset was selected. The measured common panel shows no aggregate transfer. Measurement requires at least 20 pair reads and at least 90% dominance among all retained annotated canonical alternatives sharing the boundary in every animal. RR23 flight and collection day are aliased, so this comparison cannot establish causal flight effects or functional injury.
