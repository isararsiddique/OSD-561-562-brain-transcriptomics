(a) The complete family comprises 923 clusters; 319 pass RRRM2 coverage and 281 (30.44%) meet both cohort measurement gates. Each animal requires ≥20 reads across the fixed alternatives and ≥90% dominance among all retained annotated canonical alternatives sharing the boundary. (b) Every common-cluster RR23 FLT–GC native contrast norm and its held-out directional effect. Unit Euclidean directions were learned solely from nine RR23 FLT and nine GC animals, without significance selection. No cluster passes BH or BY across all 923; failed measurements retain p=1. Tcaf1 is a historical illustration, not a selected validation family. (c) Kernel density displays of 2,000 whole-animal bootstrap draws: solid, held-out animals with directions fixed; dashed, both cohorts with RR23 directions re-estimated. Orange marks the observed equal-cluster contrast (-0.277 pp); dotted line marks zero. Conditional percentile 95% interval [-0.984, 0.371] pp; two-cohort interval [-1.061, 0.739] pp. Exact positive-direction p=0.73625 across all 11,200 stratified assignments (absolute two-sided p=0.521875). (d) All 19 held-out animals, centered by a common stratum mean only for display; points are animals and ticks are group means. Strata contain 5/3, 2/3 and 3/3 FLT/GC animals with weights 8/19, 5/19 and 6/19. Blue denotes FLT and orange GC. Positive projections mean alignment with RR23; percentage points scale a linear composition projection, not percentages of genes altered. Clusters and bootstrap draws are not independent biological replicates. RR23 flight and collection day are aliased; this comparison identifies neither a causal flight effect nor functional injury.
