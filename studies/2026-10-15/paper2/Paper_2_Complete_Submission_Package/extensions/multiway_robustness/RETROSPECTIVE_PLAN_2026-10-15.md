# Additional retrospective RR23 multiway robustness family

Written 15 October 2026 before generating any robustness outcomes. Author: Isarar Siddique. Affiliation and email blank. This extends the same RR23 animals and existing 923 frozen clusters; it is neither independent confirmation nor prospective preregistration. Original 2,769 local tests, 40 flight–ground and three flight–vivarium BY findings (41 unique clusters), and collection-date confounding are already known. No original finding will be replaced by a new favorable result.

Inputs resolve to the NEW npj package: ../../inputs and sibling ../multiway_splicing. All outputs remain in this extension. Record input hashes. Retain actual 26 animals and inherited counts/definitions, exact whole fixed-size label spaces (48,620; 24,310; 24,310), and 10^-12 tie tolerance. No biological replicate creation or candidate tuning.

1. **95% dominance local sensitivity.** A cluster survives only if its original retained alternatives account for at least 95% of all canonical boundary counts in every animal. Keep the complete family of 923×3 tests; assign p=1 to any failed cluster for all three contrasts. A passing cluster uses its original composition and exact local statistic; the gate is group-label invariant. Recalculate BH/BY over all 2,769 entries, and report every original finding's status.

2. **Ten full-matrix depth thinnings.** Independent Binomial(original count, 0.5) for every junction-by-animal cell, seeds 2026101501 through 2026101510, NumPy PCG64. Thin the ENTIRE count matrix once in each replicate, then reuse each junction's thinned count at every overlapping boundary. Save all ten full matrices in original row order, with original input hash and seeds. Freeze all original alternative lists; do not requalify junctions, replace clusters, or tune proportions. A measurement is valid if selected total≥20 in every animal and selected/all canonical boundary dominance≥90% in every animal. Failed measurements receive p=1 in the fixed 2,769-test family. Recompute local sqrt compositions and exact local p for every passing cluster, BH/BY over 2,769 within each replicate. Global tests use equal weights over the valid frozen-cluster subset, report its size, and Holm3 within each replicate. Report all ten replicate results and retention frequencies for the original 43 significant contrast–cluster pairs/41 unique clusters, including Tcaf1. These perturbations assess read-depth sensitivity; they do not create new biological replication or test mapping uncertainty.

3. **Whole-animal bootstrap uncertainty.** Use 2,000 bootstrap replicates, seed 2026101520, sampling animals with replacement within each of the three original groups at original group sizes. Each replicate's sampled animal weights are shared across every cluster and comparison. For every original cluster and all three contrasts report observed Hellinger centroid distance, bootstrap percentile interval, and a centered-error 95% confidence-ball-derived distance interval: bootstrap error is the norm of (bootstrap sqrt-centroid difference minus observed difference), divided by sqrt(2); project the 95th-percentile error radius to [max(0, observed−radius), min(1, observed+radius)]. These are pointwise approximate bootstrap uncertainty intervals, not simultaneous multiplicity-controlled declarations. Nonnegative distance percentile intervals have bias and cannot be used as new significance tests. Also report signed native-composition mean-difference percentile intervals for every retained alternative and comparison. Save all bootstrap effect values and resampling weights. Recheck existing leave-one-animal-out effect ranges using the inherited fixed definitions.

4. **Dispersion and studentization diagnostics.** For each comparison compute the within-group sample-size-unbiased mean squared radius in sqrt-composition space, averaged equally over clusters (divide by 2 to express squared Hellinger units). Enumerate labels and recompute group centroids/radii for every assignment; statistic is the absolute difference of unbiased radii. Also report a Welch-type global centroid statistic T/(V_A/n_A+V_B/n_B), with both group variance terms recomputed for every assignment. Holm3 is applied separately to the three dispersion diagnostics and separately to the three studentized-centroid sensitivity tests. Supply local/whole-arm descriptive dispersion tables for all clusters. Label enumeration is exact arithmetic under exchangeability of full distributions; it is NOT an exact equal-dispersion test when group locations differ, and studentization with n=8–9 cannot guarantee validity under unequal covariance. These diagnostics cannot prove that a centroid finding is independent of dispersion, remove the date alias, or establish causality. Interpret dispersion magnitudes and uncertainty together, retain all nulls, and avoid using nonsignificant dispersion to claim equal dispersion.
