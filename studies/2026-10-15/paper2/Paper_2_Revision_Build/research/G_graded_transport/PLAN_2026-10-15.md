# G_graded_transport: retrospective analysis plan (written 15 Oct 2026, before running)

**Status.** This analysis is retrospective. The global transport results (all four Holm p = 1) and the per-cluster
external effects were already known when it was planned. It cannot be a confirmatory test. It is reported as a
retrospective, label-exact sensitivity analysis.

**Question.** A frozen RR23 signature may fail to transfer as a whole and still transfer where the RR23 evidence was
strongest. Does the external projected effect of a cluster increase with the strength of its RR23 evidence
(evidence-graded transport)?

**Inputs (read only).** `extensions/comparator_transport/results/{RR10,RRRM2}_frozen_measurements.npz`,
`RR23_only_frozen_directions.csv.gz` (frozen unit vectors and comparator means), and
`extensions/multiway_splicing/tables/local_exact_results_complete.csv.gz` (RR23 exact p values). The analysis uses no
new data and changes no frozen quantity.

**Per-cluster external effect.** The same score as the published transport analysis:
u·(external fraction − RR23 comparator mean), summed over alternatives. It is weighted by the observed FLT/GC
allocation, with stratum weights for RRRM2. Clusters failing the external gate are excluded.

**Primary statistic (four tests).** Spearman ρ between the RR23 evidence −log10(exact p) for the training contrast and
the external effect, across measurable clusters. One test per cohort (RR10, RRRM2) and training direction
(FLT−GC, FLT−VIV). The alternative is one-sided ρ > 0. The exact null recomputes every cluster's external effect, and
then ρ, under every within-stratum allocation (252 for RR10, 11,200 for RRRM2). p = proportion of null ρ at least as
large as observed, including the observed allocation. Holm correction covers the four tests.

**Secondary (descriptive, no new inference claimed).**
(1) Mean external effect in the top decile of RR23 evidence minus the rest, with the same exact null.
(2) Spearman ρ between RR10 and RRRM2 effects on the common clusters (external–external concordance).
(3) Five visual views of the same per-cluster data (scatter, hexbin, density contours, marginal distributions,
2D density heatmap), as an exploratory figure.

**Interpretation rules, fixed in advance.** A Holm-significant positive ρ will be reported as evidence that transport
is graded by discovery evidence, not as replication of any single cluster. A non-significant ρ will be reported as
absence of detectable graded transport, not as proof that there is none. Flight/date aliasing limits all causal
interpretation either way.
