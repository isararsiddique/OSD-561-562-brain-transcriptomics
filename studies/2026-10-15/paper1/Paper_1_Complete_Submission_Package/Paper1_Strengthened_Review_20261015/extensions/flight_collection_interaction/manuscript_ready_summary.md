Metadata sensitivity excludes any animal with a location disagreement in either regional deposit. This removes all older FLT ISS-T animals from cerebellum and leaves one in hippocampus, reducing the support for an age-adjusted interaction. The coefficient assumes a common additive age effect and cannot resolve age-dependent interactions in the empty design cell. Differences between strata are estimated directly; significance in one stratum and nonsignificance in another are not evidence for interaction. Biological interpretation remains observational, and an interaction cannot isolate microgravity from collection history or other cohort-linked factors.
