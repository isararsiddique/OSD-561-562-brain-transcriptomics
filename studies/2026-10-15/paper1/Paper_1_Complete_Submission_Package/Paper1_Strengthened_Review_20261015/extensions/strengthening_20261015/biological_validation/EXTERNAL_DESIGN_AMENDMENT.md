# External design amendment before expression analysis

15 October 2026. This amendment records design facts after inspecting acquired metadata and before estimating external expression effects. It does not choose datasets from their results.

- OSD-352 RR-3 bulk Brain: six independent animals, three flight and three ground controls, one library batch. The anatomical label is Brain, so it is an external whole-brain comparison and is not equated to hippocampus, cerebellum or the original left cerebral hemisphere. It is independent of the original three missions but uses the same RR-3 mice as published RR-3 spatial/multiome work; those assays are not additional replication animals.
- OSD-202 Earth brain: forty independent animals in radiation × unloading × collection-after-treatment (one/four months) cells; smallest cell has two animals. Estimate radiation at normal loading, unloading without radiation and their direct interaction separately at each collection time, equal-time averages and late-minus-early differences. Keep all these contrasts and all sixteen frozen endpoints. Retain sequencing-lane adjustment as an additional complete sensitivity because lanes partly align with time/exposure cells. Empty or unit-leverage lane-adjusted designs have unavailable uncertainty. Time since treatment cannot be separated from changing animal age or collection context. These Earth effects are never pooled with spaceflight effects.
- OSD-374 and OSD-611 Earth hippocampus: retain the deposited two-animal pool as the independent unit. Four exposure pools and four sham pools are expected from the available design; verify against final metadata. OSD-374 has mapped member IDs; OSD-611 members are unavailable. Inferential units remain pools, and the nominal two animals per pool are never counted as independent expression replicates. Assess radiation-versus-sham context without calling it a flight replication.
- Compare all eight panels across flight contexts using both each deposit's retained definition and an additional same-anatomy common retained-gene definition. The external Brain deposit remains its own anatomical group. Direction-concordance screens are descriptive intersection–union summaries; no pooled meta-analysis is justified across the nonexchangeable mission/collection estimands. Add all newly computed component tests and derived screen entries to the complete exploratory correction family before interpretation.

Do not reproduce an unverified radiation dose conversion. The acquisition metadata retain source values; exact units must be read from ISA/protocol records before making a dose claim. No dose is needed for the binary exposure contrasts above.

## Final metadata clarification, 15 October 2026

The preliminary four-exposure/four-sham expectation above applies to OSD-374 only. Verified OSD-611 primary metadata retain ten pools: five at 0.3 Gy Si28, two at 0.6 Gy and three sham. The acquired all-twelve sensitivity adds one 0.6-Gy pool and one sham pool. The analysis uses separate dose contrasts and exactly these final masks; it does not use the preliminary balanced-group expectation. Each pool remains one inference unit, with donor IDs unavailable. This clarification preserves the earlier expectation as development history rather than presenting it as the final design.
