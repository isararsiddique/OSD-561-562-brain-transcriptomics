# Frozen biological robustness protocol

Frozen before estimating this extension on 15 October 2026. Existing reported Circadian and globin results are known; this is an explicitly exploratory follow-up, not retrospective preregistration.

1. Include every eligible independent mouse-brain processed count dataset supplied by the acquisition audit. Include all seven existing deposits as comparison contexts. Collapse technical libraries by deposited animal identity, use stable Ensembl gene IDs and the bundled release-112 symbol mapping, and retain canonical genes with mean raw count at least 10. Use median-log-ratio normalization and log2(normalized count+1), separately within deposits. Export complete sample accounting, source hashes, design support and missing panel constituents.
2. Estimate all eight inherited panels without changing their constituents. Retain the previously fixed seven erythroid-associated markers (Hba-a1, Hba-a2, Hbb-bs, Hbb-bt, Alas2, Slc4a1, Gypa) and their mean as exploratory composition context. They are not a cell-fraction estimator. No new gene set will be chosen using observed effects. Broad curated sets will only be added with a fixed independent source and a documented amendment before their analysis.
3. Compare flight with its mission-matched ground control separately by tissue and collection context. Adjust documented age or other nonredundant deposited nuisance terms where supported. RR-23 flight versus HGC is the matched-habitat comparison; VGC is retained as a separate control sensitivity. Do not call regional observations independent missions. Earth radiation/unloading contrasts will be estimated from their actual factorial design and never combined with spaceflight into a common causal exposure estimate.
4. For each core region, retain full-data and all-region metadata-conflict exclusions. Re-estimate flight-by-collection effects within each age, an equal-age average of age-specific effects where both ages have all four exposure-by-collection cells, the original additive-age interaction, and the age difference in interaction. Empty cells are unavailable, not extrapolated. For inference require at least two animals per contributing cell; unsupported estimates may be shown descriptively with unavailable uncertainty.
5. Quantify composition sensitivity by adding the fixed erythroid score to supported core designs, with its complete design support exported. For individual marker outcomes omit the target marker from this covariate. Treat adjusted effects as descriptive statistical sensitivity: conditioning on a possibly exposure-associated marker is not causal mediation or proof of contamination. Report absolute coefficient change, partial R2 and residual marker associations. Do not convert a bulk marker score into cell proportions.
6. Use whole-animal robust HC3 uncertainty and restricted-model Rademacher wild inference for supported designs (99,999 draws), plus 2,000 whole-animal stratified bootstrap repetitions. Keep model singularity and unit-leverage flags. All newly performed panel/marker/score tests across deposits, estimands and sensitivities form one complete exploratory BH family; retain BY sensitivity. Unavailable p values remain unavailable. No favorable subfamily or discovery-only output will be created.
7. Export every one-gene omission for all panels and every one-animal omission for all estimable panel and fixed-marker scores, including rank failures. Report influence as descriptive sensitivity, without generating a selected new discovery family. Examine age-specific effects, marker adjustment and omissions even if they reduce corrected support.
8. Harmonize cross-cohort effect comparisons by tissue and estimand. Report directions, intervals and heterogeneity across all eligible comparisons. Only meta-analyze a clearly compatible flight-minus-matched-ground estimand for the same tissue; require at least three independent missions and report prediction uncertainty. Otherwise show descriptive between-context differences and refrain from a pooled discovery. Shared animals and collection-duration differences remain explicit.
9. Independently verify important effects using direct group-mean or dense-regression calculations, an alternative sandwich implementation and independent BH recomputation. Save executable code, source hashes, full result CSVs, verification output, a candid report and a journal-style multipanel figure from actual saved results.

This protocol does not seek a predetermined significant outcome and cannot repair unmeasured collection variables, nonrandomized exposure, or unsupported design cells.
