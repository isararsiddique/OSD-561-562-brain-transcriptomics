## Design

2 × 2 factorial, n = 3 ROIs per cell per region: Spaceflight (Flight vs Ground Control) × Treatment (BuOE antioxidant vs saline).

## The deposited statistics cannot be used as they stand

- The deposited `Adjusted pvalue` is below the deposited raw `Pvalue` for 77–78% of targets. No multiple-testing correction can lower a p-value.
- It takes at most 101 distinct values (rounded to two decimals).
- Counted as deposited, 1,317–2,361 targets per contrast look significant. Recomputing Benjamini-Hochberg from the raw p-values gives **0** across the entire experiment; the smallest adjusted p-value anywhere is 0.129.

## Differential expression

- Targets at BH < 0.05 and |log2FC| ≥ 1.0: **0**

## Does BuOE attenuate the spaceflight response?

| Region | RMS effect, saline | RMS effect, BuOE | saline − BuOE (95% CI) | bootstrap p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CA1 | 0.101 | 0.081 | +0.0037 (+0.0008, +0.0067) | 0.013 | attenuation supported (borderline) |
| DG | 0.119 | 0.071 | +0.0091 (+0.0066, +0.0117) | < 0.001 | attenuation supported |
| FCtx | 0.081 | 0.066 | +0.0022 (+0.0000, +0.0044) | 0.047 | attenuation supported (borderline) |
| Ctx | 0.040 | 0.064 | -0.0025 (-0.0045, -0.0006) | 0.010 | amplification supported (borderline) |

No individual target reaches FDR significance for the interaction term in any region, so this is an aggregate effect-size comparison, not a gene list. The BuOE arm carries 1.09–1.42× the within-arm variance of the saline arm, so the choice of variance estimate matters: the verdict changes for CA1, DG, FCtx, Ctx when a single pooled variance is assumed instead.
