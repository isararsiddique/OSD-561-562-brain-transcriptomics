# Limitations and proposed mechanistic validation

## Limits of the completed study

| Constraint | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Original terminal bulk preservation differs by exposure | Flight and preservation effects cannot be uniquely separated |
| RR8 processing day perfectly tracks exposure | A biological flight response cannot be isolated from day/handling |
| Original GeoMx animal links are unresolved; slide/group confounding | ROI counts cannot be treated as verified animal N or causal rescue evidence |
| Small donor cohorts and limited exact permutation spaces | Strong modeled q-values may coexist with uncorrected exact tests |
| Shared regions, modalities and cell-QC variants | Repeated views do not add independent biological replication |
| RR23 habitat/vivarium and collection-date differences | Comparator-specific results limit general flight claims |
| State training and testing share mice | Numerical states and fitted effects remain exploratory |
| Missing or sparse donor/states | Fixed-composition and finer-state comparisons may be unavailable |
| Inferred RNA fractions and enriched populations | Fractions are not absolute cell counts; enrichment is not pure cellular identity |
| Multiple exploratory branches | Family-level BH does not correct across every analytical choice |
| RNA versus function | Transcript abundance cannot establish ASC assembly, caspase activation, cytokine release, membrane rupture or EV cargo |

## Proposed next experiment

The immediate hypothesis is opposing sensor/adaptor RNA regulation, with cell-mixture and handling explanations kept as alternatives. None of the following laboratory steps is completed in this repository.

1. Use an independently collected, adequately powered cohort with matched preservation, processing day and habitat controls. Specify animal identities and planned contrasts before analysis.
2. Identify myeloid populations independently of the outcome genes, and quantify absolute abundance as well as RNA expression. Separate microglia and other myeloid populations when supported by measurements.
3. Measure AIM2, PYCARD, IL18 and NINJ1 RNA alongside protein abundance and ASC assembly/caspase/cytokine readouts in the same defined populations.
4. Test membrane rupture and relevant inflammatory responses before perturbing AIM2/adaptor pathways. Perturbation/rescue should distinguish transcript divergence from altered pathway function.
5. Validate regional transfer in a separately collected mission/control context; retain prespecified negative results and comparator-specific effects.

The candidate mechanism is weakened if the RNA pattern follows collection day, disappears in well-covered independently identified populations, or fails protein/activity confirmation. Power should be calculated for the proposed endpoint and design from justified variance estimates; this study does not supply a validated universal effect size for planning all assays.
