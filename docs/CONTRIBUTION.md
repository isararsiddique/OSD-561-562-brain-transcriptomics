# Five-axis contribution and comparison with prior work

The five axes are an assessment rubric, not five proven novel mechanisms or a numeric novelty score. The study uses established assays and algorithms. Its added value is the source-linked, animal-aware evaluation of a corrected panel across control, QC, state and regional alternatives.

| Axis | Established precedent | Added analysis here | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biological question | Roy 2025 protein endpoints; Grandke 2026 RR8 molecular responses | Opposing sensor/adaptor/execution RNA directions with alternative explanations tested | Functional uncoupling and protection unproven |
| Measurement / contrast | Masarapu 2024 multimodal brain study; Suzuki/Uruno Nrf2 work | Corrected targets across habitat/vivarium, genotype, region and article/GEO QC definitions | No new assay or cross-assay quantitative equivalence |
| Method | DESeq2, edgeR, voom, PCA/K-means, NNLS/ridge, CAMERA are established | Complete families, non-panel exposure-blind states, all-mouse refits and technical sensitivity | No new algorithm or validated new cell taxonomy |
| Statistical evidence | Prior broad brain/pathway associations in their own designs | Independent family recalculation, influence failures and full q distributions alongside positive effects | No correction over every exploratory choice or causal identifiability |
| Cellular / mission specificity | Public deposits reuse donors and missions | Identity-aware 68 additional different mice; RR23 AIM2 deletion stability; regional failures retained | No universal pan-brain transfer or independent mission proof of the full program |

## Closest primary studies

**Masarapu et al., 2024** already integrate RNA/ATAC and spatial transcriptomics. Multimodal integration itself is established. This reanalysis adds panel-specific donor-level contrasts and explicitly separates spatial localization from exposure inference.

**Grandke et al., 2026** already analyze the RR8 study. Reusing its mRNA data is not an untouched-data discovery. The contribution here is the target-specific engine/QC/state sensitivity and documented article/GEO threshold discrepancy. A complete author-versus-current supplementary result comparison was not performed; no claim is made that the authors missed these genes.

**Roy et al., 2025** report ASC/caspase-1/IL1β protein endpoints. A generic first inflammasome decrease claim is therefore unjustified, and our RNA pattern does not measure activation. Submitted protein-source flags remain an assay audit.

**Kremsky et al., 2023** study the original antioxidant intervention. RR3 spaceflight-only data cannot establish antioxidant efficacy. Original GeoMx design/identity constraints remain visible.

**Suzuki et al., 2020 and Uruno et al., 2021** establish MHU-3/Nrf2 spaceflight and brain biology. Here the new work is a selected-panel, complete-family control/interaction analysis using all deposited animals and explicit omission sensitivity. No corrected Nrf2 protection interaction is established.

These are qualitative comparisons of question, design and evidence. Different preprocessing, samples and correction families prevent a fair ranking based on p-values alone. Primary references and access limits are in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md).

## Defensible contribution statement

“An animal-level reanalysis identifies context-dependent sensor/adaptor RNA divergence in enriched forebrain and tests sensitivity to cell filtering, captured state composition, control selection and regional transfer. Additional cerebellar AIM2 support is stable to mouse deletion, while finer-state, spatial and genotype-interaction evidence limits a universal or causal interpretation.”
